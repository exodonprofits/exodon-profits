# ADR-001 — Exodon Business Model

## Status
Accepted for validation

## Context

Exodon considered building standalone SaaS products such as industry-specific applications.

The market contains many mature SaaS competitors. Competing directly would require customer acquisition, feature parity, support, and long-term product investment.

Exodon already has experience building custom operational tools such as Salon Genie around real business workflows.

## Decision

Exodon will initially operate as an **AI Business Systems company** using a Smart Layer model.

The company will:
- retain useful client software
- configure or integrate existing systems where appropriate
- build missing capabilities
- add AI and automation where measurable value exists

## Consequences

### Positive
- clearer differentiation than generic SaaS
- can solve client-specific pain
- can reuse existing modules
- can generate project revenue plus recurring support
- customer discovery may reveal future SaaS opportunities

### Negative
- risk of excessive customization
- support burden
- difficult scoping
- architecture discipline required

## Guardrail

Custom does not mean from scratch.
